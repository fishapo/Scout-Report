# External Scout-Report Structure Review

The platform should not copy one form verbatim. It should absorb recurring data concepts from multiple contemporary scouting structures and keep them configurable.

## UW–Madison field scouting template
Useful concepts include grower/farm/scout/field/date/crop, row width, crop stage and height, plants per area, repeated survey stops, weed pressure plus average/max height, insect counts or damage, insect life stage/size notes, disease infection percentage, disease plant-part location, notable field conditions and field-map areas of interest.

## Current digital scouting templates
Current digital examples add field identifier, crop growth stage, overall plant health, soil moisture, weed pressure, pest/disease presence, structured detailed observations and comments. Another current crop scouting form includes plant height, air temperature, wind, cloud cover, insect and disease findings, weeds, plant population, good plants and photos/signature.

## Agri-Data-style field scouting
Useful additions include crop stage, weather, seeding depth, root development, weed identification, insects, irrigation comments, recommendations and image upload.

## Kenya-oriented scouting/surveillance
KALRO guidance emphasises scouting frequency/pattern, pest presence and population, disease and weed monitoring and using observations to guide control decisions. Kenya PP&FSD describes surveillance/reporting and ICT-based plant-protection information management as operational functions.

## Design conclusion
The canonical platform should therefore support both:
1. a simple quick scout flow for ordinary visits; and
2. a detailed survey mode for investigations, outbreaks, research, audits or high-risk observations.

The form should reveal advanced modules only when the crop/program/configuration calls for them.
